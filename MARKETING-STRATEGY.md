# Peel Excavation Services — Business & Marketing Strategy

## Client Deal
- **Total contract:** $300 for the website
- **Payment structure:** $150 deposit (received), $150 on delivery
- **Additional arrangement:** Commission on leads generated after launch — percentage TBD, must be agreed in writing before final delivery
- **Timeline:** 2 days to build, 30-day marketing trial after launch

## Important Context
The client is not paying for a website as a business asset — he is paying to generate leads. The website is the tool. Leads are the product. Every design decision should serve conversion, not just aesthetics.

**Photos:** Real client-supplied photos are in `website photos/` folder inside this project. Use these throughout the site. Client will supply more over time.

---

## Full Action Plan

### 1. Website Build (2 days)
- 4 pages: Home, About, Blog, Contact
- SEO baked in during build — keywords throughout, meta tags, schema markup, fast load
- Mobile-first — Instagram and Google traffic is almost entirely mobile
- Contact form goes directly to client phone via email or SMS
- Form fields: Name, Phone, Service (dropdown), Message
- Trust signals throughout: WSIB, licensed & insured, years experience, service area

### 2. Google Business Profile (free, do this first after launch)
- Business name, phone, website, service area: Mississauga / Brampton / Caledon
- All 5 services listed
- Real project photos uploaded
- Get client to collect 5–10 reviews from past customers immediately
- Highest probability source of actual leads at zero cost

### 3. Instagram & Facebook Setup
- Create both accounts, link website in bio
- Do NOT buy followers — fake followers tank engagement rate, which makes paid ads perform worse and cost more
- Post 5 real project pieces using photos from `website photos/` folder
- Use location tags: Mississauga, Brampton, Caledon
- Hashtags: #excavation #mississaugacontractor #bramptonhomes #peelregion #demolition

### 4. Boost One Post (real ad spend, ~$50)
- Pick the strongest project video or photo
- Run as a paid boost on Instagram/Facebook targeting:
  - Peel Region postal codes
  - Homeowners aged 30–55
  - Interests: home renovation, construction, real estate
- Expected reach: 3,000–5,000 local impressions
- Do NOT buy fake engagement — hurts ad performance, wastes budget

### 5. SEO Audit After Launch
- Run an SEO audit once the site is live
- Fix any issues flagged
- Monitor GBP for search impressions and clicks

---

## Realistic Expectations
- **GBP** — highest chance of a real lead, zero cost
- **Boosted post** — builds local awareness, may generate 1–2 inquiries
- **Organic Instagram** — trust signal for people who find the business elsewhere, not a direct lead source
- **SEO** — long game, 3–6 months before meaningful organic traffic
- If nothing after 30 days, the marketing trial ends with no further commitment
