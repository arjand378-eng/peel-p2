> **Last updated:** 2026-04-30 — site is live on Cloudflare Pages (`peel-p2.pages.dev`), custom domain not yet purchased. This doc reflects the *real* business situation discovered post-launch, which is more complicated than the original deal.

## The Real Business Structure (important context)

This is **not** a standard client/contractor relationship. Three parties:

- **Jasper** — the actual excavation operator who does the work. Owns the real business, license, insurance, GBP (if any).
- **Manny Dhanoa** — Arjan's friend, acting as a **lead-gen middleman** for Jasper. Plans to take a commission on jobs Jasper closes from the website.
- **Arjan** — built the website + would do marketing, taking a cut of Manny's commission.

**Key implication:** Jasper does not know (or does not participate in) the website/marketing setup. Manny is running this as his own lead-gen funnel that passes leads to Jasper. This means:

- **Google Local Service Ads (LSA) is likely off the table** — requires Jasper's license/insurance verification + background check
- **Google Business Profile (GBP) is likely off the table** — requires Jasper's business verification (postcard/phone)
- **HomeStars** — same problem; requires real verified contractor identity
- The only realistic paid channels left: **Meta Ads** and **Google Search Ads** driving traffic to the website, with leads captured via the contact form and forwarded to Jasper

This dramatically narrows the marketing playbook and changes budget math.

## Risk Assessment

1. **Manny is hesitating on $150 + $20 domain** — predictor that ad spend reimbursement / commission flow will also be friction.
2. **Manny is speculating** — he has no signed deal with Jasper, is hoping leads materialize so he can prove value. He needs Arjan to front time/effort on the bet that it pays off later.
3. **Three-layer payment chain:** Customer → Jasper → Manny → Arjan. Every layer adds default risk.
4. **Whole funnel is fragile:** if Jasper cuts Manny out, the website's value evaporates and Arjan doesn't get paid further.
5. **No contract = no recourse** if anything goes sideways.

## Realistic Ad-Budget Math at $150–$200/month

| Channel | $150 buys | Realistic leads | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search Ads | 6–15 clicks total | 0–1 | **Skip.** Budget too low for algorithm to train. |
| Meta Ads (FB/IG) | 30–75 clicks | 0–2, low quality | Marginal. Workable for brand awareness. |
| Google LSA | 2–5 leads if eligible | 2–5 | **Best paid option** — but needs Jasper. Probably blocked. |
| HomeStars | Below their min ($200–300) | — | Off the table at $150. |
| **Free / organic** | $0 | 0–8/month, compounding | **Best ROI** but needs GBP, which needs Jasper |

**Conclusion:** at $150–200 ad spend, with no Jasper involvement, **paid lead-gen is mostly futile.** Meta is the only honest option, and it'll generate 0–2 weak leads in month 1.

## Recommended Action Plan (in order)

### Step 1: Settle the website balance ($150)
Before anything else. If Manny pays without drama → real client. If he pushes back → predictor of everything to come. **Do not start ads / blog / equipment page / SEO audit until this is paid.**

### Step 2: Domain on Manny's card
Non-negotiable. Don't front it. If he won't buy a $20 domain for his own lead-gen project, that itself is the answer about whether he'll fund ads.

### Step 3: Surface the Jasper question to Manny
Arjan needs honest answers before doing more work:
- Does Jasper know the website exists?
- Is there a real agreement (verbal or written) on commissions?
- Can Manny get Jasper's cooperation for GBP / LSA verification, or do we have to assume that's blocked?
- Who owns the phone number on the site? The email?

### Step 4: If everything checks out, do FREE work first during the trial month
Highest-leverage uses of Arjan's free month (in order):
1. **Fully populate Google Business Profile IF Jasper participates.** This single move outperforms any $150 ad spend.
2. **Get 5+ Google reviews** from past clients (Manny coordinates with Jasper).
3. **Free directory listings:** HomeStars free profile, Houzz, Bing Places, Yelp Canada.
4. **On-page SEO** on the existing live site (use `seo-audit` skill).
5. **Blog content** for SEO topic authority (use `blog-write` skill — already configured).

### Step 5: Only if Step 4 produces something AND the $150 is paid → spend ad budget
Best $150 paid deployment, in priority:
- **LSA** (if Jasper verifies) → 2–5 leads
- **Meta Ads to website** with AI-generated static creative (Nano Banana / Gemini for free imagery) → 0–2 leads, brand awareness
- **Skip Google Search Ads entirely** at this budget tier

### Step 6: Set realistic expectations with Manny IN WRITING
Before any ads run, send him this in text/email:
> "For 1 month at ~$150 ad spend, realistic outcome is 2–8 total leads combined across organic + paid. Month 1 is always the worst — algorithms aren't trained yet. Trades marketing usually takes 3 months to stabilize. This is a trial, not a verdict."

This protects against being blamed for a small-budget result.

## The Walk-Away Threshold

Arjan should be willing to walk if any of these happen:
- Manny doesn't pay the remaining $150 within a defined deadline
- Manny refuses to put domain on his own card
- Manny can't / won't answer the Jasper question
- Manny expects Arjan to front ad spend on his own card

In all those cases: hand over a clean copy of the live site as the deliverable, keep the $150 deposit, treat the project as a portfolio piece, and move on. Better than sinking 40+ more hours into a relationship that won't pay.
